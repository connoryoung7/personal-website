Hey, I’m Connor: a product-minded software engineer with experience across a wide range of technologies. I studied Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon, and since then I’ve pretty much lived in the startup world. I’ve worked at companies across the whole spectrum — a tiny founding team, fast-growing seed and Series A startups, and a Series D unicorn that became one of the fastest-growing digital health companies ever. The pace was chaotic at times, but I’ve always found the energy of startups exhilarating and enjoyed building things from scratch.

I enjoy building software, because it’s a way of participating in the invisible infrastructure of modern life — adding small pieces to the systems that power how we live, work, and connect. After discovering programming, I gained a general curiosity and appreciation for how everything meshes together.

Most of my work has been in the healthcare space, where the problems are complex and the impact is real. I've built a wide range of applications, from frontend React apps to distributed systems in Go. Lately, I’ve been diving into AI and homelabbing — sometimes merging the two when it leads to something interesting.

If you ever want to chat about startups, engineering, or AI ideas — or if you’d like a copy of my résumé — feel free to reach out.
